Notes on reliable systems

When you plan a migration, you should take into account the performance implications before you carry out the change.

In the event of a network failure, the system will fall back to the cached response.

This improves overall reliability and reduces the risk of unexpected failures.

The parser stays maintainable, provided that public interfaces remain small and explicit.

A new application can still be based on the old format as a result of the compatibility promise, as opposed to a full rewrite.

Out of the box, the reader will not replace the page text.
